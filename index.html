2. What information is processed?

Game progress and preferences. Block Grove saves information such as scores, unlocked levels, achievements, selected themes, and settings on your device so that gameplay can continue. The App does not require an account to play. These locally stored records are not intended to identify you and are not automatically sent to me merely because they are saved on your device.

Analytics. If enabled in accordance with your consent choices, Block Grove uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the App is used and improve its performance and design. Analytics may process app interaction events (such as opening the App, starting or completing game modes and levels, or other gameplay events), session information, app version, device model, operating system, approximate geographic information, and an app-instance identifier. The exact events processed depend on the version of the App and the analytics settings in use.

Advertising. Block Grove uses Google AdMob to display ads, including banner ads, interstitial ads, and rewarded ads that you may choose to watch in exchange for in-game benefits. Subject to applicable permissions and consent, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device and app identifiers, IP address, approximate location, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver, measure, and protect ads. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your choices and the available ad-serving options. Non-personalized ads may still involve limited data processing.

Support inquiries. If you email me for bug reports, help, or privacy inquiries, I process your email address and any information you include in the message to respond and resolve your request. Please avoid sending sensitive information unless necessary.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the relevant purposes and legal bases are:

  • Save game progress and operate the App: legitimate interests in providing the game and maintaining its essential functionality (Article 6(1)(f)), insofar as personal data are involved.
  • Optional analytics and advertising-related storage or processing requiring consent: your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), collected where required through the App's privacy choices. Additional ad processing may rely on legal bases identified by advertising partners and explained in the consent interface.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests: legitimate interests in handling correspondence (Article 6(1)(f)), or compliance with a legal obligation when responding to statutory privacy requests (Article 6(1)(c)).

You can decline optional consent without losing access to the core game. Certain advertising functions, including availability of rewarded ads, may be limited if ads cannot lawfully be requested or served.

5. Consent and your choices

Where required, a consent form will be shown through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can choose whether to consent to applicable advertising and analytics purposes. You can revisit relevant choices in the App via Settings → Privacy Options once that feature is implemented.

Where required by Apple, Block Grove will request separate App Tracking Transparency permission before tracking across other companies' apps or websites. Declining this permission does not prevent you from playing the game.

Important: This section describes the intended release configuration. Privacy Options, Analytics consent defaults, and advertising gates must be implemented and tested in the App before publishing this policy.

6. Retention and deletion

Locally stored game progress and settings normally remain on your device until you reset the relevant information, erase the App's data, or uninstall it, subject to your device's backup settings.

Analytics and advertising information may be retained by Google and its partners according to the applicable service settings and retention policies. Before publication, verify and document the actual retention settings for your Firebase Analytics property and the relevant ad services. Support messages are kept only for as long as reasonably needed to answer the inquiry, resolve related issues, or meet applicable legal obligations.

You may ask me about deletion of information under my control by emailing knstudio.blockclear@gmail.com. Because the App does not currently use user accounts, I may not be able to associate all pseudonymous data held by third-party providers with a particular person solely from an email address. I will explain available options and assist as reasonably possible.

To exercise your rights, contact knstudio.blockclear@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. In Austria, this is the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde).
